# BNBEra MVP completion audit — 9 September 2026

**Verdict: the MVP is not concluded.** BNBEra is a deployed, working marketplace with real testnet settlement and mainnet paid-delivery evidence. It is substantially beyond a prototype. However, the public category journey currently has no enabled hires under rebalancing or yield, the planned discovery schedule is absent from the inspected user crontab, and the deployed historical Greenfield links are unavailable. The full current-wallet acceptance and final submission package are also not established.

This is three different conclusions:

- **Main-track readiness: incomplete.** Public access and real BSC supply exist; equal category depth, decision-useful current data and a demonstrated low-friction activation path across the categories remain materially short.
- **The repository's expanded T1–T9/G1–G4 MVP: incomplete.** Historical local/testnet successes remain valid, but they do not close current production integration and T9 acceptance.
- **Partner eligibility: separate.** TermiX's report is missing from the inspected repository; Altana has historical testnet evidence but needs a reproducible submission proof and current product walkthrough. Neither Greenfield nor a completed seven-day mainnet settlement is an explicit main-track prerequisite.
